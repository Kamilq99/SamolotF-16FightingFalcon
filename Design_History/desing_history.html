Design History

The F-16 Fighting Falcon is one of the most recognizable and versatile fighters in military aviation history. Its history dates back to the early 1970s, when the United States Air Force initiated a program to search for a modern, lightweight air superiority fighter. This project, known as the Lightweight Fighter Program, was created in response to experience gained during the Vietnam War...

As a result of a competition that included prototypes from various companies, the General Dynamics design was the winner — an aircraft designated as the YF-16. The prototype first flew on January 20, 1974 and proved to be a breakthrough...

From the very beginning, the aircraft was designed as a day fighter, light and cheap to operate, intended to complement the more expensive and heavier F-15 Eagle...

The F-16 turned out to be a huge export success — to date, over 4,600 units have been delivered to the air forces of over 25 countries, including Poland...

Despite the passage of more than four decades since its first flight, the F-16 has not lost its relevance...

The history of the F-16 is a history of engineering vision, military need and consistent technological development...
